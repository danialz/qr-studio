# Security policy

QR Studio is a client-side application. It does not collect or upload QR contents, files, or camera frames.

For security-sensitive issues, please avoid sharing exploit details publicly until maintainers have had an opportunity to assess them. Contact the maintainer through the contact information on [danialz.com](https://danialz.com).

**Scope:** unsafe URL handling, cross-site scripting, malicious image handling, permissions, and dependency vulnerabilities. Scanned QR content is untrusted input; never auto-navigate to unverified destinations.
